Your space. Your data.

Privacy notice · Updated 15 October 2026

What stays on your device

Your workspace, meal diary, recipes, nutrition profile, plan and notification settings are saved in this browser’s local storage. There is no shared user database or cross-device sync. Other people using the same browser profile and app address may be able to see this data. Clearing browser storage removes it. Food data can be exported from Food & movement.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google, the app requests access to events on calendars you own and uses your primary calendar to display the next two weeks and create or edit events you review. Google’s permission wording also includes deletion, but the app does not offer event deletion. Your access token and retrieved events remain in this tab’s memory, are not saved in local storage, and are not sent to the AI assistant. Refreshing or expiration requires reconnecting. Disconnect clears the local session and requests Google revocation. You can also remove access at Google Account connections.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only for these user-facing Calendar and reminder features, not advertising, sale or AI training.

Food and health details

Age, height, weight, food logs and preferences stay in this browser. Built-in food search runs locally. If the site owner enables USDA FoodData Central search, only the food search words go through the app’s server to USDA, not your profile or diary. Nutrition values and generated plans are estimates for general planning, not clinical care.

Optional AI

When configured, submitted assistant messages go through the server to OpenAI. Workspace context is shared only when you select “Include my workspace.” Calendar events and your food profile and diary are excluded from that optional context. Information you type into a message is part of the submitted message. API requests use store: false. Messages remain in memory for the current tab.

Notifications

Notifications require your browser permission. The app checks reminders while it is open, including in a background tab subject to browser limits. No server-side push scheduler runs after closing it. You can turn categories off in Calendar & reminders or revoke browser notification permission.

Hosting and service providers

Vercel hosts the app and its optional server functions. Hosting providers may process standard request metadata such as IP addresses and server logs under their own policies. Google, USDA and OpenAI apply their own service policies when you use those integrations. The app does not add advertising or analytics trackers.

Manage your data

You can edit or remove workspace items and meals in the app, export food data, and clear this site’s browser storage to remove local data. Disconnect Google separately to revoke access. Contact the app owner at eeshita7546@gmail.com for privacy questions.
